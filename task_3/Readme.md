# 🚗 Car Sales Data Analysis (Intermediate Level - ShadowFox Internship)

This project focuses on analyzing a **Car Sales Dataset** to uncover patterns, trends, and insights related to sales performance, pricing, and manufacturer contributions.  
It is part of the **Intermediate Level Task** for the **ShadowFox Data Science Internship**.

---

## 📁 Dataset Information
The dataset contains information about car sales, manufacturers, price, sales figures, resale value, and other attributes that influence the automobile market.

---

## 🎯 Project Goals
- Perform **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on the car sales dataset.  
- Discover patterns related to sales distribution, manufacturers, and pricing.  
- Visualize important metrics for better interpretation of the automobile industry trends.  
- Derive insights that highlight market performance and resale behavior.  

---

## ❓ Research Questions Explored
- Which manufacturers record the highest car sales?  
- What is the relationship between car **price** and **sales figures**?  
- How does **resale value** vary across different manufacturers?  
- Which cars have the **highest and lowest sales**?  
- How do features like **engine size** and **horsepower** impact sales?  
- Which manufacturers dominate the automobile market in terms of **units sold** and **average price**?  

---

## 📊 Visualizations & Analysis
The analysis includes the following charts and summaries:
- **Sales by Manufacturer** – Bar Plot  
- **Average Price by Manufacturer** – Bar Plot  
- **Sales vs Price Relationship** – Scatter Plot  
- **Sales Distribution** – Histogram  
- **Correlation Heatmap** of numerical features  
- **Resale Value Analysis** – Comparisons across manufacturers  

---

## 🧰 Tech Stack
- **Python** 🐍  
- **Jupyter Notebook** 📒 (VS Code Environment)  
- **Libraries Used**:  
  - `pandas` – Data manipulation  
  - `matplotlib` – Visualization  
  - `seaborn` – Statistical plots  

---

## 📌 Key Insights
- Some manufacturers dominate the market with significantly higher unit sales.  
- Higher-priced cars don’t always guarantee higher sales; mid-priced cars show better performance.  
- Resale values vary widely between manufacturers, highlighting brand influence on long-term value.  
- Correlation analysis shows interesting relationships between features like **horsepower, engine size, and sales**.  

---

## ✅ Conclusion
This analysis provided meaningful insights into the car sales dataset, helping to understand manufacturer dominance, pricing strategies, and factors influencing sales and resale value.  

The results can assist automobile companies, analysts, and researchers in better decision-making regarding **pricing models, sales strategies, and brand positioning**.
